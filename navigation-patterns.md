# Navigation, Header, and Footer Patterns

The header will be fixed at the top of the viewport and initially float over the hero with a deep translucent obsidian fill. After a small scroll threshold, it will gain a controlled glass surface, a fine bottom rule, and a slightly reduced vertical footprint. The left cluster contains the signal-aperture mark and “ABHINAV / RESEARCH”; the right cluster contains Research, Work, Trajectory, Capabilities, Contact, and a discrete “View GitHub” external link.

On mobile, the header preserves the mark, nameplate, and a labelled menu control. The menu opens as a full-width dark panel, traps focus while open, exposes all anchor destinations in reading order, and returns focus to the triggering control on close. Its backdrop and Escape-key behavior will be implemented without relying on pointer-only interaction.

The footer is a final dark field rather than a generic link bar. It will state “Open to research, MLOps, and fintech internship conversations,” repeat the approved email and social links, and include a small status line identifying the static GitHub Pages architecture. It will never make an unverified availability, employer, or location claim beyond the approved source material.
