# Responsive Layout Foundations

The desktop portfolio uses a 12-column fluid field with 52px outer padding at 1200px and above. The hero reserves roughly seven columns for the research statement and five for the portrait or hero visual; the following sections intentionally re-balance those spans to avoid a repetitive grid. A fixed top header remains legible over black through a translucent obsidian background and a fine bottom rule.

On tablet widths, the hero maintains a two-part hierarchy but reduces display scale and moves the portrait into a balanced lower-right stage. Work cards become two-up modules with stable media aspect ratios. On phones, the primary statement precedes the portrait, each major section becomes a single reading column, and large display typography remains expressive without creating single-word line wraps.

Section surfaces must provide the actual background contrast required by their contents: white and cloud text can sit directly on obsidian or a controlled dark overlay; no essential text sits directly on uncontrolled image detail. Every image panel will expose a descriptive alternative text and a retained visual crop that does not cut through the face in the supplied portrait.
