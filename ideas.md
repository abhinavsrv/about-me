## Chosen approach — Obsidian Precision

### Design Movement

Dark luxury digital editorialism inspired by premium industrial design, product-launch cinema, and modern Swiss information systems. The interface will borrow Apple-like discipline—clarity, quiet confidence, and attention to materials—without copying Apple layouts, marks, copy, or proprietary artwork.

### Core Principles

The site will pursue **optical simplicity**, where each screen has one unmistakable purpose. It will use **cinematic restraint**, giving typography, photographs, and research outcomes room to land against a deep black field. It will express **material precision** through translucent graphite panels, hairline rules, and subtle metallic gradients instead of decorative effects. Finally, it will foreground **intellectual signal**, translating research depth into concise, accessible structures.

### Color Philosophy

Near-black obsidian creates an immersive environment that lets white type and research visualizations carry the hierarchy. Off-white softens long-form reading; muted graphite establishes surfaces and dividers; a refined ice-blue will mark interaction, selected data states, and active navigation. The portrait will be displayed in its original warm daylight palette as a deliberate human counterpoint to the monochrome system.

### Layout Paradigm

The structure uses a **cinematic scroll sequence** rather than a centered marketing grid. The hero introduces Abhinav through an oversized statement, compact research metadata, and a precisely framed portrait. Subsequent modules shift between expansive full-bleed work imagery and dense, left-aligned information columns, creating an intentional cadence of immersion and evidence.

### Signature Elements

The recurring motifs are a glowing ice-blue signal line, glass-dark project capsules with a subtle internal sheen, and oversized typographic numerals that index research themes and selected work. The supplied portrait appears in a refined, window-like crop with a dark vignette, never repeated as generic decoration.

### Interaction Philosophy

Controls should feel engineered: immediate focus response, controlled hover lift, and no ornamental UI. Work exploration will reveal technical context progressively, while links and contact actions remain direct. Every interactive state must have a clear visual and keyboard-accessible response.

### Animation

Motion will be compositional rather than flashy: soft fades and small vertical translations at 180–280 ms, a carefully eased image scale up to 1.015 on hover, and a deliberate signal-line sweep only at major section transitions. All motion will respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and no perpetual animation will appear.

### Typography System

**DM Sans** will provide precise, geometric display headlines at large scale, while **IBM Plex Sans** will handle body copy, navigation, technical labels, and project metadata. Hierarchy comes from contrast in scale, weight, letter spacing, and opacity—not an overabundance of font styles. White display type is reserved for primary narrative moments; grey technical labels remain compact and tracked.

### Brand Essence

**Abhinav Srivastava builds and studies reliable machine-learning systems for consequential real-world decisions.** The personality is analytical, exacting, and composed.

### Brand Voice

Headlines should be clear, ambitious, and evidence-led. Calls to action should sound like invitations into the work, not conversion prompts; microcopy should clarify method or context. Example headline: “Reliability is a model behavior, not a promise.” Example CTA: “Examine the research.” Generic filler such as “Welcome to my website” and “Get started today” is prohibited.

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark will be an abstract signal aperture: a small, open black-and-white square interrupted by a precise ice-blue beam. It evokes a lens, an inference window, and a research signal. It must be text-free, instantly visible at header size, and paired with a custom-set name rather than a default-font wordmark.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Ice — #A9D6FF**. This pale technical blue will be used sparingly against obsidian black for active states and measured emphasis.

## Style Decisions

The primary wordmark always reads **Abhinav Srivastava** as the authored identity; “research systems” remains a secondary descriptor. Signal Ice is treated as a scarce measurement event—one decisive emphasis per major section, plus active states, key numerals, and the recurring signal-aperture beam. Abstract imagery is paired with evidence-oriented overlays such as grids, inference windows, and calibration-like traces so visual moments reinforce research behavior rather than generic hardware luxury.
